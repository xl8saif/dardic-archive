Indus-Kohistani is written in a modified Arabic script. Beyond the shared letters, the established orthography uses a set of additional characters — several from Arabic Extended-A — to record sounds that the inherited script does not distinguish. This page records the characters themselves; precise usage descriptions will be added as they are verified against the orthographic record and community practice.

## Typography

These characters are rendered on this site in **Scheherazade New**, a Unicode-compatible Arabic-script typeface with full coverage of Arabic Extended-A. Urdu material on this site uses **Noto Nastaliq Urdu**; the established faces for Indus-Kohistani work are Scheherazade and Noto Nastaliq Urdu.

## A note on method

For an endangered language, the writing system *is* digital infrastructure. Each character settled today is a character that can be searched, typed, taught and archived tomorrow — which is why orthographic decisions are recorded carefully, with their evidence, rather than improvised.
