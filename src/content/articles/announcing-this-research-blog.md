This site is the working record of two kinds of labour that belong together: research on the languages of the Indus Kohistan, and the daily professional practice of translation and localization.

## What you will find here

Everything published here carries one of these labels:

- **A — Original research** — questions I investigate directly
- **B — Personal observations** — what I saw and heard, clearly separated from established fact
- **C — Translation** — translated material, with the source identified
- **D — Commentary** — opinion, marked as opinion
- **E — Documentation** — field records, word lists, texts
- **F — Interviews**
- **G — Archival material**
- **H — Project reports** — what was done, for whom, at what scale
- **I — Professional case studies**
- **J — Language resources**

Personal interpretation will be marked as such — never presented as established linguistic fact.

## Languages

The site publishes in **English**, **اردو (Urdu)**, **العربية (Arabic)**, **Indus-Kohistani** and **Shina**. Each language gets correct direction (RTL/LTR) and appropriate typography: Noto Nastaliq Urdu for Urdu, Amiri for Arabic, and Scheherazade New for Indus-Kohistani, whose orthography includes extended Arabic-script characters such as ڇ څ ݜ ڙ ݨ.

## The connection

Language research + preservation + translation + localization + digital language technology: each strengthens the others. Research finds the words. Orthography fixes them. Typography renders them. Localization and language technology put them to work. A language survives when it can be written, read, taught — and processed digitally.

*[FiKR&CD](https://fikrcd.vercel.app/) — the Forum for Indus-Kohistani Research & Cultural Development — is the research home for much of this work.*
